Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions.

How do payments and milestones work?
Every project runs on a transparent 50/50 model: a 50% deposit to confirm your development sprint slot, and the remaining 50% upon final verification and live deployment handover. Retainers are billed monthly in advance and can be paused or cancelled at any time.
What technology stack do you build with?
We use Astro, modern semantic HTML5, clean CSS, and TypeScript. By default, our sites ship zero unneeded client JavaScript, ensuring sub-second loading speeds. For content management, we connect Sanity, Decap CMS, or Astro Content Collections.
Can I start with a single-page site and expand later?
Yes. Our architecture is modular by design. You can deploy a Single-Page Launchpad ($950) today, then add CMS blogging, multi-page templates, or interactive calculators down the road without rewriting the existing codebase.
Do I need to supply complete designs or copy?
If you have existing brand assets and copy, we build directly from them. If you only have a concept or rough notes, we structure your messaging hierarchy, typography, and layout logic during our initial discovery pass.
Where is the website hosted?
We set up your deployment pipeline on Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, or Netlify under your own account. Hosting for static sites on these platforms is typically $0/month for standard traffic tiers, giving you zero monthly overhead.
